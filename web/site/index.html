LTTP Online

A Link to the Past in your browser, built on the zelda3 reimplementation.

Load your ROM

Pick your own copy of the US ROM (.sfc or .smc). It never leaves your computer: the game assets are extracted here, in your browser, and kept in this browser's storage so you only do this once.

Ready

Game assets are stored in this browser.

Controls

D-padW A S DStartG
AJSelectF
BML / RQ / E
X / YI / LTurboTab (hold)

Arrow keys, Enter and Right Shift also work. Gamepads work too. Your in-game saves are kept in this browser. Save states: Shift+F1…F10 to save, F1…F10 to load.

No game data is hosted here. You need your own legally obtained ROM. zelda3 is MIT licensed (license).
